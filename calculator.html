How to perform
1. Do real math with the crowd's numbers until a clean total is on screen (e.g. 3,000,000).
2. Single-tap the top-left corner to arm. The app grabs that total, computes the date-time target, and secretly loads the difference.
3. Tell spectators to "mash any number keys — totally random." Every tap enters the next hidden digit; the HUD shows how many remain.
4. When the HUD says the reveal window is open, press =. The screen shows today's date & time exactly.
5. Read it back: the HUD spells out the date/time for you.
