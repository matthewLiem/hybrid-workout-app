Recalibrate when

These go stale. Redo them after any race, after a 10K time trial, or if a threshold session at 4:38 starts averaging more than 4 bpm below 170 — that means the pace has become too easy and the whole table should shift down.

Deload every third week

Eleven sessions a week puts you squarely in the group the concurrent-training research flags as at-risk. Every third week: cut volume 40–50%, keep the intensity. Drop one easy run, halve the accessory sets, and make Friday the "pure strength-endurance" format instead of a compromised run.

The one rule that matters most

Run first on every double, and leave 6+ hours before the lift. Never lift legs heavy in the 24 hours before a quality run. Everything else in this program is downstream of those two lines.
